## My Philosophy

I am not trying to become an expert overnight. I am aiming for a marathon, not a race.

I am starting from the basics and trying to understand things properly instead of simply memorizing commands or using tools without knowing what they do.

I will make mistakes. I will get confused. Some topics will probably take me a long time to understand, but currently the only thing that i have in my hands is time.

I am going to document those things instead of hiding them.

The goal of this repository is to look back in the future and see how far I have come.
